# UGREEN Nexode Charger GaN USB C 100W

## 4 in 1 Charger
UGREEN USB C charger with 4 ports (3 USB C and 1 USB A) supports total output up to 100W, compatible with almost all devices. Charge 4 devices simultaneously and avoid tangled wires, perfect for home and work use.

## UGREEN 100W Charger
100w charger supports PD 3.0/2.0, PPS, Galaxy Super Fast Charging, QC 4+/4.0/3.0/2.0 and SCP fast charging protocols for compatibility with a variety of devices.

## Simultaneous Fast Charging
The USB-C (C1 and C2) ports provide a total power of 100W when used alone, delivers power for your laptop (65W) and mobile (30W) when both USB-C (C1 and C2) ports are used together, and the three output ports provide simultaneous 45W + 30W + 22.5W charging to 3 devices. USB-A port supports up to 22.5W when used alone, supports Huawei SCP fast charging.

## Safe and reliable
UGREEN USB C charger equipped with a smart chip to protect your devices from short circuit, overvoltage, overheating and over-current. UL94-V0 high fire resistance and robust PC housing ensure fast and safe charging.

## Wide Compatibility
100W charger can charge your devices at full speed and save you time, compatible with MacBook, Vivobook 15.6, HP Spectre, Huawei MateBook, iPad Pro/Air and tablets, iPhone 17/ iPhone 17 Air/ iPhone 17 Pro/ iPhone 17 Pro Max 12-16 series, AirPods, MagSafe and Galaxy series, Huawei MATE series, Xiaomi, Redmi, Google Pixel 8/7 , Steam Deck
